Desk lab set-up

  1. Print the lab mat on A4 at 100 % size (not "fit to page"). Lay it flat on a table.
  2. Put the phone on a stand at the near edge of the mat, sideways (landscape), about 40 cm from the middle of the mat and tilted so the back camera looks down at it (about 45°). The screen faces you; you reach past the phone to the mat.
  3. All 6 black squares should be on screen. Then the glassware appears on the mat at real size.
  4. Pinch your thumb and first finger on a glowing piece to hold it. Open them to let go. Tap Calibrate hand once with your hand flat on the mat.
